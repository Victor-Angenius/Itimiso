01 · Story

The day Opportunity stopped talking

In 2018, a dust storm engulfed Mars and blotted out the sky above Opportunity. Its solar panels were starved of light. Its final message read like a report of a weak battery and fading light.

For months, the team on Earth sent thousands of commands. No answer came. In February 2019, the mission was declared over. A robot designed to work for 90 sols had worked for about 5,000.

One sol is one day on Mars, roughly 24 hours and 40 minutes.

03 · What they found

A Mars that was once wet

The rovers and landers found minerals that only form with the help of water, the floor of an ancient lake, and marsquakes that help reveal what lies inside the planet. All of it points the way for where the next mission should go.
