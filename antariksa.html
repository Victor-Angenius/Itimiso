01 · Story

A golden record for anyone who finds it

Both Voyagers carry the Golden Record, a disc holding greetings in many languages, music, sounds of nature, and images of life on Earth. If someday someone out there finds it, they will know a little about us.

In 1990, Voyager 1 turned its camera around to photograph Earth from billions of kilometers away. The result was a tiny dot caught in a beam of light, and the photo became known as the Pale Blue Dot.

Voyager cannot turn around. It can only keep telling its story for as long as its power lasts.

How far is that?

One astronomical unit (AU) is the distance from Earth to the Sun, about 150 million km. Voyager 1 is now more than 160 times that distance away from us.
