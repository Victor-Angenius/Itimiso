02 · Why leave them behind?

Coming home costs more than going

Bringing a lander home means carrying extra fuel, and that makes the spacecraft far heavier. That is why engineers chose to leave behind equipment that had finished its job, and instead bring home something more valuable: rocks and data.

Some of that equipment then went silent. Others, like the laser mirrors on the Moon or Voyager in interstellar space, kept working for decades.

One kilogram left behind meant one kilogram of moon rock that could come home.
